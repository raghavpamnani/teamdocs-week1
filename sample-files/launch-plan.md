# Northstar launch plan

## Goal
Ship a document workspace that keeps teams organized without compromising access boundaries.

## Acceptance checklist
- Members can upload a document and find it in the library.
- Administrators can approve the document and review its audit trail.
- Viewers can download documents but cannot modify or export the register.
- A user from another workspace cannot access this document, even with its ID.
- Workspace records can be exported as CSV.
